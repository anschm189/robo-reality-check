Robot-Reality Check: Measuring Progress Against Sci-Fi Dreams

A workshop at ICRA 2027 — Seoul, Korea

Half-day Talks + Panel + Poster Session

About

How close is your robot to sci-fi? This workshop brings together researchers across robotics domains to benchmark current capabilities against the archetypes science fiction has long depicted — and to treat those same stories as cautionary tales about surveillance, labor, and equity. The central deliverable is a shared capability table: a structured, comparable assessment of what today's systems match, where hard limits remain, and what we should actually want going forward.

Capability Axes

1.Learning & Decision-Making (e.g., Johnny 5 in Short Circuit)
2.Hardware & Actuation (e.g., exoskeleton in Elysium)
3.Manipulation & Physical Assistance (e.g., Baymax in Big Hero 6)
4.Human Perception & Trust (e.g., Robot & Frank)
