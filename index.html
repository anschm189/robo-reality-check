// how close is your robot to sci-fi?

Robot-Reality Check

Measuring Progress Against Sci-Fi Dreams
Half-day workshop at ICRA 2027 — Seoul, Korea

14:00–18:00  afternoon session 4 capability axes 5 expert talks 20 early-career posters

01About

Each invited speaker holds their own system against a fictional archetype from their domain — the autonomous vehicles of Minority Report, Baymax, the exoskeletons of Elysium, the humanoids of Ex Machina. Together we discuss what current systems already match, where hard limits remain, and what we should actually want going forward — treating fiction not only as a target, but as a cautionary tale about surveillance, emotional labor, and equity. The central deliverable is a shared capability table: a structured, updatable benchmark of the sci-fi robotics gap, built in the room with the audience.
