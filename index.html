Throughout the afternoon, the audience fills in a shared table built at the front of the room: for each axis, what current systems already match, where hard limits remain, and in the final panel, what we should actually want going forward. We aim to summarize the workshop findings in a report or paper and the resulting capability table is designed to be updatable: each future edition can be compared against the previous one, turning the workshop into a small longitudinal benchmark of the sci-fi robotics gap.
